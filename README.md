Started & Local InstallationLicense🌟 OverviewMohaserei Network (MSN) & Ecosystem is a production-ready, interactive digital web platform built for community economic empowerment, collective capital pooling, and transparent profit distribution in the Kingdom of Cambodia.The platform features a live bilingual toggle (ភាសាខ្មែរ / English), an interactive Member E-Wallet Simulator, dynamic Chart.js financial allocations and projections, and strict adherence to fiduciary governance and data privacy.🏛️ Core Pillars & ArchitectureCollective Digital Economy: Pooling grassroots capital and micro-savings ($10 per investment unit) to build community-owned assets.4-Sector Investment Fund: Capital diversification across real businesses (SMEs), regulated stocks/securities, commercial real estate, and safety reserve funds.Integrated Member E-Wallet: Real-time ledger tracking cash balances, owned units, transaction histories, and automated dividend payouts.Transparency & Audit: Periodic financial reporting, independent audits, risk disclosures, and mandatory KYC verification.💳 Interactive E-Wallet & Dividend SimulatorThe platform includes a fully reactive dashboard simulator where users can:Test depositing simulated funds via KHQR / Bank Transfer.Purchase investment units ($10/unit) and track their updated portfolio balance.Use sliders to adjust owned investment units and fund net ROI rates to instantly preview monthly, quarterly, and annual dividend earnings (60% member profit share).📊 Investment Fund Structure & Profit SharingFund Capital Allocation:40% — Real Businesses & SMEs (Daily cash-flow generation)30% — Stocks & Regulated Securities (Liquidity & appreciation)20% — Real Estate & Commercial Properties (Long-term growth & rentals)10% — Safety Reserve Fund (Treasury liquidity & member protection)Net Profit Sharing Formula (60 / 20 / 20):60% — Distributed directly to members as periodic dividends (Monthly / Quarterly / Annual).20% — Reinvestment / Compounding to scale collective fund assets.20% — Platform operations, cybersecurity, technology development, and audits.📈 Long-Term Financial Projections (2030–2052)MilestoneTarget YearActive
